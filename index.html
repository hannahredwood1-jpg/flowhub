FAQ

Questions.

Do I need trading experience?

No. The Trading School goes from A to Z. Start at the very beginning, work through each level and its exam, and finish with the official model and your certificate of completion.

Is this a signal group?

No. FLOWMTD is a school. Daily levels and trade recaps show the model working in real time, but the goal is for you to understand every trade and become a trader who can find them on your own.

What market do you trade?

NQ futures, across the Asia and New York sessions.

Can I copy your trades or automate them?

No. Trades are shared so you can learn from them. You make every decision and enter every trade yourself.

What is the Mentorship?

The Mentorship is the highest level of FLOWMTD. You learn a far more advanced, order-flow version of ECHO x ORBIT, the way it's actually traded at the top level, with direct 1-on-1 coaching and reviews of your own trades. It also includes personal development: the discipline, habits and mindset that keep you consistent once the stakes and the payouts get bigger.
