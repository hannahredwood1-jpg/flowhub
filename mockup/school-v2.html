What's different from today's school

  • Two looks, same app. Modern is the default. Playful keeps the HUD feel and adds ranks and XP. Flip any time with the switch at the top.
  • Teaching first. Every lesson is learn → see it annotated on a chart → mark it yourself → explain it in writing → exam.
  • TradingView-style chart with a crosshair, OHLC legend, price axis, drawing tools, pan/zoom and a time-frame switch. Neutral candles by default, as your curriculum recommends.
  • Exams that mean something. Charts and numbers are generated fresh each attempt. No feedback until you submit. Fail and the module restarts.
